# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The preferred audience is engineers and engineering leaders looking for ideas they can apply in their own work, particularly around software practice, technical leadership, and AI-assisted development.

## Product Purpose

The site is primarily a publication. An article should leave the reader with an idea, lesson, or technique they can use. The strategic goal is to establish John Wilger's reputation as an expert in his field. About and Projects support the writing by showing who John is and what he's worked on. Publication-first is a preference, not a fixed scope boundary.

## Positioning

The writing makes engineering judgment visible through specific decisions, constraints, tradeoffs, and evidence from the work. The [editorial guidance](docs/writing.md) favors concrete lessons over generic marketing claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Readers can browse the [article archive](content/blog/), follow RSS or Atom feeds, and listen to narrated articles when audio is available. The [About](content/about.md) and [Projects](content/projects.md) pages provide professional context and point readers to John's work.

## Evidence on Hand

The published articles and linked projects show John's work and reasoning. John hasn't seen evidence yet that the site is building his reputation. No success metric or measurement approach has been chosen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**User requirement:** The site must meet WCAG 2.1 AA. This requirement is not a claim that the current site has been audited or conforms.
