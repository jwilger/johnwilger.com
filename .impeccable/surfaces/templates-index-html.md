# Homepage

Mode: Persuade. Article pages are Read: preserve published text, URLs, feeds, and article illustrations. Synthetic narration is archived, not published.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Lead with a real person and a specific question of engineering judgment, rather than a tech-themed showpiece or résumé.

**OWN-WORLD:** Natural alpine portrait, blue-gray ground, deep slate ink and quiet Bodoni titles. A layered ridgeline borrows from the photo; thin rules organize writing. Use the same blue-gray ground for reading, without decoration behind prose.

**STORY:** Recognize John as Head of Engineering at 10kR, learn how he thinks about shipping AI-assisted work, then read an essay or find the archive, About, and Projects.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** On desktop, name and short statement left, square portrait right, layered ridge below the copy and left of the photo. On phones, photo first, then introduction, then ridge. One explicit essay link leads into the first section. No animation: the visual signature is the static echo of the portrait, not a timed reveal.

**FORM:** Code-led portrait profile; seed: none (pinned), approved with the stronger ridgeline: “ok, lets roll with this approach”.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
