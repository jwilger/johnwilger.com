# Archived synthetic narration

The website no longer offers synthetic narration. These are the existing audio files, kept outside Zola’s `static/` directory. The original credit and source metadata remains in the article front matter as a historical record and does not render. `ignored_static` in `zola.toml` also prevents future files under `static/audio/blog/` from going into a production build.

The renderer and its isolated audio pipeline tests are retained. Do not restore the player or publish new narration without a separate decision about the voice and publication treatment.
